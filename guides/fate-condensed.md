# Fate Condensed

During roleplay, Chertia primarily uses a modified version of Fate Condensed to determine the success of certain actions and dialogues. Here is generally how it works.

### Characters

If you've ever played Chertia or a TTRPG before, you should be familiar with the character creation process. A handout is provided to players to draft their characters, and then storytellers will work with players to adjust.

### Aspects & Statuses

Aspects are key information about your character that define their personality, behaviour, and abilities. They are self-defined. The only ask is that you don't create an overpowered character; other than that, you're free to create any aspects. We ask that you include 3 to 6 aspects, and try to include at least 1 negative aspect.

Aspects can change, disappear, and be added throughout the course of a character's development. Aspects are always true, but 'invoking' them during roleplay or an event might cost you a dice roll... or more.

Here are some examples of aspects:

* "Smooth-talker" - helps the character barter, talk their way out of dangerous situations, etc.
* "Afraid of conflict" - can come up in so many different ways; they might not speak up for themselves, they might run away from physical fights, etc.
* "Scar on left cheek" - physical aspect that might intimidate other characters or indicate that your character has been through battle.
* "Clumsy" - we better hope your character isn't asked to hold anything important.
* "Must protect X" - if your character knows another character, you can create aspects that form dynamics.

Statuses are temporary aspects. This is likely self-explanatory, but statuses affect a character's immediate abilities. In Fate Condensed, these care called Consequences. These can both help and hinder your character. **You do not submit any of these with your character.** Trust is given to the player to apply statuses on their own (but the storyteller can apply them as well).

Here are some examples of statuses:

* "Left arm is broken" - your character cannot use their left arm at all.
* "Stuck under a rock" - might be literally, might be metaphorically.
* "Suffering from debilitating self-doubt" - this might be applied to your character after they fail miserably.
* "Blinded by rage" - your character's actions will be fuelled by anger.
